## Reading [ours]

1. **Inner CE: grid and history both help and stack.** Grid 1 → 2 → 4: 3.450 → 3.410 → 3.382. History 2/4.8/10 s:
   probe 3.440 → 3.401, ResNet 3.530 → 3.364. Grid4 + past 2/4.8/10 s is the best arm (3.307 ± 0.022). One past
   frame at 2 s adds nothing on top of the grid (3.387). For the ResNet, inner CE prefers more context
   (1.2 s 3.489, 2 s 3.473, 4.8 s 3.446, three 3.364).
2. **None of it is outcome prediction.** Outcome+clock CE does not move, so the gains are better reading of
   elapsed time.
3. **No π₀.₅ probe reads task progress.** No probe arm has a positive milestone excess on all 3 seeds, the jump
   does not separate outcomes (AUC 0.46–0.57), and success MAE after 20 s stays 0.155–0.178 vs the ResNet's
   0.119–0.128. Every ResNet arm is positive on 3/3 seeds (+0.057 … +0.079, AUC 0.62–0.71).
4. **Correction to the 2026-10-29 dip entries ("grid removes the dip", "it is the features, not the head").** Both
   rest on seed 0. The probe outcome head's behaviour at placement is not stable across seeds: grid4 P dip +0.159 /
   −0.101 / +0.003, mean-pool P dip −0.074 / +0.134 / +0.081, grid1 (attention on per-image means) P excess
   −0.060 / −0.163 / −0.069. With 3 seeds neither the dip in the mean-pool probe nor its removal by the grid is
   established, so the confound question has no answer at this resolution.
