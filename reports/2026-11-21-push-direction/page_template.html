What a push is

At every replan the frozen base π0.5 proposes an action chunk. The guided policy changes it by w · (h₊ − h₋), the residual's "good minus bad" direction. The push is the difference between the guided chunk and the base chunk, from the same noise draw at the same state.

Per pair of policies: cos is 1 for the same direction, 0 for unrelated, −1 for opposite. β is how much of the second policy's push the first contains (1 = all of it). Both are scored on the arm-motion push: 5 executed steps × (xyz + rotation), normalised as the model sees it. The gripper is scored separately (hover a row). In normalised units it carries about two thirds of all push energy, almost all of it in the few frames around grasp and release, and it would swamp the arm. Measured on .
